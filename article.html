History of Japanese Drift Cars

Nissan Skyline

Nissan Skyline is one of the most popular cars for drift. First released in 1957, the Skyline became known for its power, technology, and good handling.

The history of the Skyline spans decades, evolving from a simple family car to a recognized supercar that captured the hearts of car enthusiasts worldwide.

Toyota AE86

Toyota AE86, also known as "Hachi-Roku," is a drift legend. This compact car was released in 1983 and quickly became popular among racers due to its lightweight and ability for controlled drifting.

The AE86 became an icon for drifters and continues to captivate the imagination of car enthusiasts worldwide with its legendary reputation and heritage in the racing world.

Mazda RX-7

Mazda RX-7 is another popular car for drift. Introduced in 1978, the RX-7 stood out with its rotary engine and excellent handling, making it attractive to racers.

The RX-7 remains one of the most successful drift cars in history, winning numerous victories on tracks and serving as a symbol of advanced technology and design.
